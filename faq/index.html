Mobile service FAQs

Answers about REVLINE’s service area, mobile appointments, diagnostics, pricing and what to send with a request.

Request serviceCall REVLINE
Where does REVLINE provide mobile mechanic service?

REVLINE lists Provo, Orem, Lehi, American Fork, Pleasant Grove, Saratoga Springs, Lindon, Draper and Spanish Fork. Submit the exact address so route and job availability can be confirmed.

Does REVLINE come to my home or workplace?

Mobile appointments can be requested at a home or workplace. The site and repair must be safe and suitable; REVLINE confirms location access before dispatch.

Is my online request a confirmed appointment?

No. The form sends a service request and preferred time. REVLINE must contact you to confirm vehicle fit, service scope, location and appointment.

How do I request a quote?

Submit your name, phone, email, city, vehicle year/make/model, service and issue description. Add photos if they clarify the concern.

What information helps diagnose a car problem?

Describe when the symptom occurs, dashboard warnings, recent repairs, sounds, starting behavior and any scan codes. Avoid replacing parts based only on a code.

Can a check-engine code tell me which part failed?

A code identifies a system or condition; it does not automatically prove a specific part has failed. Testing and vehicle context matter.

What is the starting price for diagnostics?

The site lists mobile diagnostics from $100. Vehicle, fault, access, travel and work scope can affect the final quote; REVLINE confirms the price before work.

What does electrical diagnostics start at?

Electrical diagnostics are listed from $120. The reported symptoms, vehicle and diagnostic time affect scope, so ask REVLINE to confirm the estimate.

How much is brake service?

Brake service is listed from $100. Parts, vehicle configuration and the condition found affect the total; request a vehicle-specific quote.

How much does battery replacement cost?

Battery replacement is listed from $120. Vehicle-specific battery type, access and any required registration or adaptation can change the final amount.

How much does starter or alternator service cost?

The website’s starting price example is $150. The exact vehicle, diagnosis, part and repair scope determine the final quote.

How much is an oil change?

Oil changes are listed from $75. Oil specification, capacity, filter and vehicle access can change the final price.

What does programming and coding start at?

Programming and coding are listed from $150. Compatibility depends on the vehicle, module and software access; REVLINE reviews these details first.

How are RV and trailer jobs priced?

RV and trailer work receives a custom quote because configurations, systems, parts and access differ. Send photos and describe the system involved.

Does REVLINE work on European vehicles?

REVLINE accepts requests for supported European vehicles, including makes shown on the website. Send year, make, model and the requested operation so compatibility can be confirmed.

Can REVLINE work on Audi, BMW or Mercedes-Benz?

Submit the exact model, year and concern. REVLINE reviews vehicle and operation support before confirming a diagnostic or repair appointment.

Can you diagnose an electrical drain or a dead battery?

Send the symptoms, how long the vehicle sits, any warning lights and recent battery or accessory work. REVLINE will confirm whether mobile testing is suitable.

Can you service a vehicle that will not start?

Share whether the engine cranks, clicks or is silent, plus the vehicle and location. REVLINE reviews if an on-site no-start diagnosis is a fit.

Do I need to tow my vehicle to a shop?

Some concerns may be handled where the vehicle is parked; others need shop equipment, parts or safer access. REVLINE reviews the specific issue before recommending a plan.

Can I upload photos with my request?

Yes. The service form accepts up to three JPG or PNG photos with a combined size under 10 MB. Do not upload personal documents or payment information.

Can I text photos instead?

Yes. The website’s Text REVLINE button opens a text message to the listed business number; whether your phone supports image messaging depends on your device and carrier.

Can I pick a date and time online?

You can request a preferred date and time window. The request is not a reservation until REVLINE confirms it directly.

How soon can REVLINE come out?

Availability depends on the current schedule, location, vehicle and parts. Submit your preferred window and REVLINE will confirm what is possible.

Does REVLINE do fleet service?

The form accepts fleet-service requests. Include the number of vehicles, location, requested work and preferred schedule so availability can be reviewed.

Can REVLINE work on trucks?

The site accepts requests for supported trucks and domestic vehicles. Provide the exact year, make, model and repair so fit can be confirmed.

Does REVLINE work on RVs and trailers?

REVLINE accepts RV and trailer service requests, including selected electrical concerns. Scope and quote are confirmed after the configuration and symptoms are reviewed.

Does REVLINE repair suspension?

Suspension repair requests are accepted for supported vehicles. Describe the noise, handling change or visible issue and provide vehicle details.

Can REVLINE replace a starter or alternator?

Starter and alternator requests can be submitted. REVLINE reviews the diagnostic evidence, vehicle and part availability before confirming repair.

Can I get an oil change at my address?

Submit the vehicle and service address. REVLINE confirms whether the location and vehicle are suitable for an on-site oil service.

What payment methods does REVLINE accept?

Contact REVLINE when confirming the estimate to ask about current payment options. The website does not currently take online card payments or financing applications.

Does the website offer Affirm or Klarna financing?

No financing application or BNPL checkout is currently connected to the website. Any future financing availability will be subject to provider approval and final terms.

Where can I read REVLINE’s Google reviews?

Open the REVLINE Reviews page and use the link to the official Google Business Profile for current ratings and review details.

How can I leave an honest review?

After service, use REVLINE’s Google Business Profile link to leave feedback based on your experience. Reviews should not be exchanged for incentives.

What if my city is not listed?

Choose Other / Not Listed in the form or contact REVLINE with the exact location. Travel and service availability will be confirmed before scheduling.

Still have a question?

Tell REVLINE your vehicle, city and concern. We will confirm the right next step.
