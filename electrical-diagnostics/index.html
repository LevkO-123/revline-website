Electrical Diagnostics questions

Can you find an intermittent electrical fault?

REVLINE can review the symptoms and vehicle details and confirm whether the reported fault is a fit for mobile diagnostics. Some intermittent faults require repeated conditions or specialist equipment.

Should I replace the battery first?

Not automatically. A battery, charging circuit, cable, connection or parasitic draw can produce overlapping symptoms; testing helps narrow down the cause.
