Help REVLINE check the job fit

Include the trailer type, make and model if known, its location, the symptom and any recent work. If the concern involves lights, wiring, battery, braking or another system, describe exactly what changed.

Photos can clarify the request

Send a wide view of the trailer and a close photo of the affected area when practical. REVLINE will review whether the trailer and requested work can be serviced safely at the proposed location.

What happens after you request service

  1. REVLINE reviews the trailer, issue and service address.
  2. REVLINE confirms whether the requested work fits mobile service.
  3. You receive the proposed scope, custom quote and appointment confirmation before dispatch.
