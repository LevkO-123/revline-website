Starter and alternator questions

Is a clicking noise always the starter?

No. Battery state, cable connections, starter circuit and other conditions can create similar symptoms. Provide the vehicle and what happens when you try to start it so the right diagnostic step can be confirmed.

Can you replace an alternator without testing it?

REVLINE reviews the charging symptoms and vehicle details first. Testing helps distinguish alternator trouble from battery, wiring or connection problems.
